How long to wait after levothyroxine before coffee?

Take it on an empty stomach and wait 30 to 60 minutes before breakfast or coffee. Keep calcium, iron, magnesium and antacids at least four hours away.

The short answer

30 to 60 minutes. Levothyroxine is absorbed best on an empty stomach, so it is usually taken first thing in the morning, with the first food or drink of the day held back for half an hour to an hour.

Source: Levothyroxine — MedlinePlus Drug Information, U.S. National Library of Medicine.

Why coffee gets singled out

Nothing about coffee is uniquely forbidden. The rule is the empty stomach, and coffee is simply the thing most people reach for within ten minutes of waking — before the tablet has had any time at all. Coffee with milk adds calcium to the same swallow, which is a second problem rather than the same one.

So the practical version is: the tablet, then the window, then whatever you normally have.

The four-hour rule is a different rule

Some things need much more separation than breakfast does. Keep calcium, iron, magnesium and antacids at least 4 hours away from a levothyroxine dose — they block absorption rather than merely slowing it.

Source: Levothyroxine — MedlinePlus Drug Information, U.S. National Library of Medicine.

This is the one people get wrong most often, because a calcium or iron supplement feels like part of the same morning routine as the thyroid tablet. If both are daily, the usual fix is to move the supplement to the evening.

Consistency beats precision

The dose you are on was arrived at by measuring your TSH some weeks after a change — usually 6 to 12 weeks after — while you were taking the tablet in whatever way you take it.

Source: Hypothyroidism, American Thyroid Association.

That means a routine you can actually keep is worth more than an ideal one you follow twice a week. If 60 minutes without coffee is not going to survive a Tuesday, a reliable 30 is the better answer — and if you take it at night instead, well after your last meal, that is a routine too. The failure mode is not "wrong window", it is a different window every day, because then a TSH result has nothing stable to be read against.

Questions people also ask

Does black coffee count?

It counts as breaking the empty stomach, which is what the window is protecting. Milk adds calcium on top of that.

What if I forget and eat straight away?

One morning is one morning; it is not a dose you have to make up or double. What is worth doing is noting it, so that if a lab result comes back odd you can see whether that week was unusual. Never change a dose yourself — that is your doctor's call.

Can I take it at bedtime instead?

Some people do, because the requirement is an empty stomach rather than a particular hour, and the evening can be easier to keep clear than the morning. Discuss the switch with your doctor first, and expect the next TSH to be the thing that confirms it worked.
