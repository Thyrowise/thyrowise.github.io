Frequently asked questions.

Short answers. If yours is not here, email aykutaydmr@gmail.com and a person will reply.

Is Thyrowise free?

Yes, and the free tier is not a trial. Logging labs, symptoms, doses and medication reminders stays free. Thyrowise Pro is optional — 9.99 USD monthly, 29.99 USD yearly with a 7-day free trial, or 59.99 USD once for a lifetime unlock — and adds the TSH trend chart against your own band, dose-impact comparison, symptom trends, the endocrinologist-ready PDF and CSV export.

Does it need an account?

No. No sign-up, no login, no server. There is no account to be locked out of, and no database of your thyroid history anywhere to be breached.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. The app never needs a connection to log or read anything.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. A backup is a plain, readable JSON file that you export and put wherever you like — Files, your own iCloud Drive, a Mac. It is readable rather than an opaque blob on purpose: a backup you cannot open is a promise you cannot check.

What if I lose my phone?

The honest answer: with no server, a lost phone is a lost history unless you exported a backup first. Restoring replaces what is in the app rather than merging, and it says so before it does it.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet — iPhone and Apple Watch today, Android in progress.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes. Mark the dose taken from the wrist; the empty-stomach countdown runs there and on the Lock Screen.

Does it connect to Apple Health?

If you turn it on. Thyrowise can read weight, sleep, steps and resting heart rate and show them beside your log. Off by default, revocable in Health, and nothing is inferred or diagnosed from it.

Which lab values can I track?

TSH, free T4, free T3, total T4, total T3, TPO antibodies, thyroglobulin antibodies and reverse T3 — each with the date drawn. Thyroid nodules and ultrasound scans too, including the TI-RADS category from the report.

Can I get my data out?

PDF for the appointment, CSV for yourself, JSON for a full backup.

Does it tell me what my results mean?

No, and that is deliberate. Insights are counts of your own logs — "fatigue on 19 of the last 30 days, against 8 in the 30 before". Every medical statement in the app names its source, and the app lists them all on a Sources screen. What the ranges are and where they come from.

What languages does it support?

Eleven: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Russian and Turkish.

Does it work for hyperthyroidism or Graves'?

Partly. Lab logging, symptoms and the trend chart apply to any thyroid condition. The medication side is built around a daily levothyroxine dose and its empty-stomach wait, so it fits hypothyroidism and Hashimoto's best.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your Apple ID settings, any time — at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. Lifetime is one-time and has nothing to cancel.
